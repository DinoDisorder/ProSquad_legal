Privacy Policy

How Lineup Bot handles information when used in Discord servers.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dino "Disorder" Mulic (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information through Lineup Bot (the “Bot”). The Bot helps Discord communities organize EA SPORTS FC Pro Clubs events, RSVPs, league fixtures, and player lineups.

Information the Bot handles

When a server uses the Bot, it handles only information needed to provide its features, which may include:

  • Discord identifiers for servers, channels, messages, and users, such as server, channel, message, and user IDs.
  • Discord display names or usernames used to label event creators, attendees, and assigned lineup players.
  • Event details supplied by organizers, such as event titles, descriptions, dates, times, time zones, event categories, tournament or league names, and opponent names.
  • RSVP choices, lineup positions, tentative RSVP indicators, and player presence or cancellation responses.
  • Server event-time-zone settings.

Organizers choose what event information to enter and which Discord channel receives the event post. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the Bot.

How information is used

We use this information to:

  • Create and update event posts, RSVP lists, generated images, and lineups.
  • Display player names, mentions, RSVP status, and lineup confirmations in Discord.
  • Save event and server settings so they remain available after the Bot restarts.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the Bot.

Backups or provider logs, if any, may take additional time to expire under the provider's retention practices.

Sharing and visibility

The Bot sends information to Discord to operate and display its features. Event and lineup posts are visible to people who can view the Discord channel where they are posted, according to that server's channel permissions. The Bot does not sell personal information. We do not currently use an EA or Virtual Pro Gaming account integration.

Payments

The Bot does not currently process payments or collect payment-card information. If paid features are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data handling before those features are offered.
